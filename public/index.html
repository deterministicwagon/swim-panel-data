Swim Schedule Data Feed

This site publishes a compact, machine-readable feed of weekend lane swims at Brewer, Minto Barrhaven, Richcraft Kanata, and Nepean Sportsplex.

The feed uses the third-party, unofficial ottrec export, which extracts data from City of Ottawa facility pages. It is not an official City repository or API.

Files

  • schedule.json — compact device feed
  • notices.json — plain-text notice details referenced by the feed

Attribution

Compiled data © Patrick Gaskin, ottrec scraper. Facility information and schedules © City of Ottawa. Additional attribution from the source dataset is preserved in schedule.json.

Disclaimer

This unofficial feed can be delayed or incomplete. Check the City of Ottawa before travelling. Sessions affected by an ambiguous notice are explicitly marked uncertain; the feed does not ask devices to parse HTML.
